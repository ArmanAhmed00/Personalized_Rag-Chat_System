# Code of conduct

Outlawz adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every
background and level of experience, give and take feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best
for the people who use Outlawz.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults or personal attacks, sexualised language or
imagery, publishing someone's private information without their permission, and any conduct that
would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/ArmanAhmed00), or open a private report from the repository's
Security tab if you'd rather not be seen asking. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban
anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor
Covenant.

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone
represents the project elsewhere.
